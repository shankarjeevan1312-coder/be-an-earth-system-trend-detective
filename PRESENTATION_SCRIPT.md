# 🎤 2-Minute Presentation Pitch Script & Storyboard

**Project:** Earth System Trend Detective  
**Format:** 120-Second Video / Presentation Deck for Judges

---

## ⏱️ Video Storyboard (0:00 - 2:00)

### [0:00 - 0:25] The Hook & Investigation Scope
* **Visual:** Dark globe animation transitioning to the Streamlit Dashboard banner.
* **Speaker:**
  > *"Every day, NASA satellites capture petabytes of Earth observation data. But when we look at individual datasets in isolation, we miss the bigger picture. We are Team Earth System Trend Detectives, and we set out to answer a critical question: How fast is Earth's interconnected climate engine really accelerating across different spheres?"*

### [0:25 - 0:55] Global Acceleration & Arctic Amplification (Tabs 1 & 2)
* **Visual:** Screen recording of Tab 1 (GISTEMP anomaly bar chart) and the Latitude-Band Heatmap.
* **Speaker:**
  > *"Interrogating 140 years of NASA GISS records, our statistical engine uncovered a striking finding: before the year 2000, warming was increasing at 0.08 degrees per decade. After 2000, that rate surged to over 0.21 degrees per decade—an acceleration factor of nearly 3x. Looking at our zonal heatmap, you can see Arctic Amplification in high northern latitudes warming at over triple the global average."*

### [0:55 - 1:25] Regional Biomes & Cryosphere Tipping Points (Tabs 2 & 3)
* **Visual:** Switching to Tab 2 showing regional anomaly spikes in South Asia & the Amazon, followed by Tab 3's Arctic Sea Ice curve.
* **Speaker:**
  > *"Using the NASA POWER Climatology API across six key global biomes—from the Amazon to South Asia—our rolling Z-score anomaly detector revealed that over 68% of extreme thermal shocks occurred in just the last ten years. Simultaneously, satellite records from NSIDC confirm Arctic September sea ice has diminished by over 3.5 million square kilometers since 1979."*

### [1:25 - 1:50] AI Forecasting & Interactive Tool (Tab 4)
* **Visual:** Demonstration of the AI Trajectory Forecasting slider and Random Forest driver weights.
* **Speaker:**
  > *"To help researchers and policymakers test future scenarios, we built a machine learning forecasting engine with 95% confidence intervals and multi-variable driver ranking. Anyone can query any coordinate on Earth in real time to investigate their local climate trajectory."*

### [1:50 - 2:00] Closing & Call to Action
* **Visual:** Team credits and repository QR code / GitHub link.
* **Speaker:**
  > *"By turning raw NASA observations into actionable intelligence, the Earth System Trend Detective makes planetary data accessible, intuitive, and decisive for our future. Thank you!"*
